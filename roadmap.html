PLANNED

The next projects

RECOVERY PRIORITY

Test and document a restore

I want to restore a guest from backup and document the steps and checks. I have not verified a restore yet.

Separate physical backup storage

I want to keep backup copies on separate physical storage so they do not depend on the laptop or its SSD surviving.

Ongoing resource monitoring

I want to track CPU, memory, and storage use over time to help plan changes. The screenshots I have now only show individual moments.

Keep improving the services

As I use the services, I want to improve how I maintain them, organize their files, and configure the devices that connect to them.

The current recovery limit

The lab and its backups are on one laptop. Losing that machine or its storage could mean losing both. I still need separate backup storage and a tested restore process; the lab also has no second host to take over if this one fails.
